Pork & Broccoli Stir-Fry over Rice

new

Serves 4 · ~15 min active · ~35 min total · reheats very well

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb pork loin, sliced thin against the grain
  • 1 lb broccoli crowns, cut into bite-size florets
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce, divided
  • 1 tbsp garlic-infused oil
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil, divided
  • 1.5 cups basmati rice + 2.25 cups water
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Rinse rice, add to a pot with the water and a pinch of salt. Boil uncovered, then cover, simmer on low 15 min, rest covered 5 min, fluff.
  2. Slice pork thin against the grain (across the muscle lines).
  3. Toss pork with cornstarch, 1 tbsp soy sauce, pepper. Rest 10 min.
  4. Cut broccoli into florets.
  5. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over high heat until shimmering.
  6. Add pork in one layer, sit 1 min, then stir-fry 3–4 min until no pink remains and lightly browned (145°F/63°C, then rest 3 min). Remove to a plate.
  7. Add remaining 1 tbsp oil; stir-fry broccoli 2–3 min until bright green and fork-tender but still a little crisp.
  8. Return pork with remaining soy sauce, garlic oil, ginger. Toss 1 min until glossy and hot.
  9. Serve over rice.

Safety: pork to at least 145°F/63°C, then rest 3 min.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days. Microwave covered 1.5–2 min, stir, 30–60 sec more.

Chicken & Yu Choy Ginger Rice Bowls

new

Serves 4 · ~15 min active · ~35 min total · reheats very well

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cubed
  • 1 lb yu choy sum, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar (or lemon juice)
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil, divided
  • 1.5 cups basmati or jasmine rice + 2.25 cups water
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Cook rice as usual (rinse, boil, simmer 15 min covered, rest 5 min, fluff).
  2. Cube chicken; season with salt and pepper.
  3. Wash yu choy, cut into 2-inch pieces, keeping stems separate from leafy tops.
  4. Whisk ginger, soy sauce, vinegar, maple syrup into a glaze.
  5. Heat 1 tbsp oil over medium-high until shimmering.
  6. Cook chicken 5–6 min, flipping once, until no pink remains and juices run clear (165°F/74°C). Remove to a plate.
  7. Add remaining oil; stir-fry stems 1 min, then leafy tops 1–2 min until wilted but not mushy.
  8. Return chicken, add glaze, toss 1 min until coated and hot.
  9. Serve over rice.

Safety: chicken to 165°F/74°C — no rest needed.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days. Microwave covered 2 min, stir, 30–60 sec more.

Beef & Bok Choy Stir-Fry

new

Serves 4 · ~15 min active · ~35 min total · reheats very well

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb beef top blade, sliced thin against the grain
  • 1 lb baby bok choy sprout, halved or quartered lengthwise
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 3 tbsp Lee Kum Kee oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil, divided
  • 1.5 cups rice + 2.25 cups water

Steps

  1. Cook rice as usual.
  2. Slice beef thin against the grain into ¼-inch strips.
  3. Toss beef with cornstarch and soy sauce; rest 10 min.
  4. Rinse bok choy, halve or quarter lengthwise, keep root end intact.
  5. Heat 1 tbsp oil over high heat until shimmering.
  6. Add beef in one layer, sit 1 min, stir-fry 2–3 min until browned (thin strips cook in under a minute — watch color, not just the clock). Remove to a plate.
  7. Add remaining oil; cook bok choy cut-side down 2 min until lightly charred, flip 1–2 min more until stalks are just tender.
  8. Return beef, add oyster sauce and ginger, toss 1 min until glossy and hot.
  9. Serve over rice.

Safety: whole-muscle beef to at least 145°F/63°C with a 3-min rest; thin strips cook through by color/texture.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days. Microwave covered 2 min, stir, 30–60 sec more.

Sheet-Pan Pork Chops & Broccoli

new

Serves 4 · ~10 min active · ~30 min total · one pan, oven-based, reheats well sliced

Source: Creme De La Crumb · 4.96★ (139 ratings). Followed as written; potatoes dropped to keep this to just chops + broccoli.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): ranch seasoning + garlic powder are concentrated onion/garlic. Kept true to source. Lighter option: swap for garlic-infused oil + a no-onion herb blend.

Ingredients

  • 4 thick-cut boneless pork sirloin/centre-cut chops (~1.5–2 lb)
  • 1 lb broccoli crowns, cut into bite-size florets
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Italian herb seasoning (or Herbes de Provence)
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp dry ranch seasoning
  • Olive oil, for broccoli (~1 tbsp) and coating chops
  • Optional: lemon wedge, for serving

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F/220°C. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment or foil if you have it.
  2. Pat chops dry, season thoroughly both sides with salt, pepper, garlic powder, Italian herbs, smoked paprika, and ranch seasoning.
  3. Toss broccoli with ~1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper.
  4. Arrange chops and broccoli on separate halves of the sheet, spread out in a single layer.
  5. Roast 18–22 min, flipping chops once halfway, until golden and broccoli has browned edges (chops to 145°F/63°C at the thickest part).
  6. Rest chops 3–5 min on a cutting board before slicing.
  7. Slice against the grain into ½-inch strips. Serve with broccoli, over rice, with lemon if using.

Safety: pork to at least 145°F/63°C, then rest 3 min.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days, sliced. Microwave covered 1.5–2 min, then 30–60 sec more.

Tomato-Braised Sicilian Pork Chops

new

Serves 4 · ~15 min active · ~2 hr 35 min total (mostly hands-off oven time) · reheats excellently, freezes well

Source: Ask Chef Dennis · 5★ (103 ratings), the strongest-rated real match found. Note: Italian basil-tomato flavor (not tex-mex), and a long oven braise rather than a quick simmer.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (high): ½ cup diced onion + 2 cloves garlic sauteed into the sauce base. Kept true to source. Lighter option: swap for garlic-infused oil + green onion tops (mutes the flavor base — a real tradeoff).

Ingredients

  • 4 thick-cut pork chops (~8 oz each, ~2 lb total)
  • 56 oz crushed tomatoes (two 28 oz cans)
  • ½ cup diced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely diced
  • 2 tsp sea salt, divided
  • 2 tsp cracked black pepper, divided
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh basil, divided
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F/163°C.
  2. Pat chops dry, season both sides with 1 tsp salt and 1 tsp pepper.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chops 2 min per side until golden. Remove to a plate.
  4. In the same pan, sauté the diced onion and garlic ~1 min, until fragrant and softened.
  5. Add crushed tomatoes, half the basil, remaining salt and pepper. Stir, scraping up browned bits. Bring to a light boil.
  6. Return chops to the pan, spoon sauce over top. Cover tightly with a lid or foil.
  7. Braise in the oven 2 hr 15 min, until completely fork-tender.
  8. Doneness: pork to at least 145°F/63°C — this braise passes that easily; judge by texture (falls apart with a fork).
  9. Re-season sauce if needed; thin with a splash of water or reduce uncovered on the stovetop if too thick. Stir in remaining basil. Serve with rice.

Safety: pork to at least 145°F/63°C — judge by texture (fork-tender) since this braise runs well past the minimum.

Storage

Fridge 4–5 days, or freeze up to 3 months. Reheat stovetop ~5 min, or microwave covered 2–3 min, spooning sauce over halfway.

Pan-Seared Salmon with Napa Cabbage

new · fresh dinner only

Serves 2 · ~20 min total · do not batch for office lunches — strong fish smell

Ingredients

  • 2 Atlantic salmon steaks (~½ lb each)
  • ½ head napa cabbage, sliced into ribbons
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp grated fresh ginger
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 cup rice + 1.5 cups water, or leftover rice

Steps

  1. Cook rice if not using leftovers.
  2. Pat salmon dry, season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  4. Sear salmon undisturbed 4 min, flip carefully, cook 3–4 min more.
  5. Salmon is done when it flakes easily and looks opaque throughout (145°F/63°C). Remove and tent with foil.
  6. In the same pan, add butter, napa cabbage, soy sauce, ginger. Sauté 2–3 min until wilted but still with a little bite.
  7. Serve salmon over rice with the cabbage alongside.

Safety: fish to at least 145°F/63°C, or opaque and flaking easily.

Storage

Best fresh. Leftovers: fridge up to 2 days, eat at home only. Microwave gently ~1 min, or eat cold.
