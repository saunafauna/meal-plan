Sheet-Pan Pork Chops & Broccoli

newbatch

Leverages · Walmart pork chops, T&T broccoli

Serves 4 (2 × 2 meals) · ~10 min active · ~30 min total · one pan, oven-based, reheats well sliced

Source: Creme De La Crumb · 4.96★ (139 ratings). Followed as written; potatoes dropped to keep this to chops + broccoli.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): ranch seasoning + garlic powder are concentrated onion/garlic. Kept true to source. Lighter option: swap for garlic-infused oil + a no-onion herb blend, or use a smaller pinch of each.

Ingredients

  • 4 thick-cut boneless pork sirloin/centre-cut chops (~1.5–2 lb)
  • 1 lb broccoli crowns, cut into bite-size florets
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Italian herb seasoning (or Herbes de Provence)
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp dry ranch seasoning
  • Olive oil, for broccoli (~1 tbsp) and coating chops
  • Optional: lemon wedge, for serving

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F/220°C. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment or foil if you have it.
  2. Pat chops dry, season thoroughly both sides with salt, pepper, garlic powder, Italian herbs, smoked paprika, and ranch seasoning.
  3. Toss broccoli with ~1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper.
  4. Arrange chops and broccoli on separate halves of the sheet, spread out in a single layer (crowding steams the broccoli instead of roasting it).
  5. Roast 18–22 min, flipping chops once halfway, until golden and broccoli has lightly browned edges (chops to 145°F/63°C at the thickest part).
  6. Rest chops 3–5 min on a cutting board before slicing — keeps juices in the meat.
  7. Slice against the grain into ½-inch strips. Serve with broccoli, over rice, with lemon if using.

Safety: pork to at least 145°F/63°C, then rest 3 min.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days, sliced. Microwave covered 1.5–2 min, then 30–60 sec more.

Pan-Seared Salmon with Napa Cabbage

newfresh-only

Leverages · Superstore salmon steaks, T&T napa

Serves 2 · ~25 min total · do not batch for office lunches — strong fish smell

Source: WellPlated · 4.54★ (267 ratings).

Modified from source: the napa cabbage side (this household's usual pairing) is added on, sautéed separately in butter, soy, and ginger; the salmon itself follows the source closely.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): garlic and honey are notable triggers. Lighter option: swap the honey for maple syrup and use garlic-infused oil instead of minced garlic.

Ingredients

  • 2 Atlantic salmon steaks (~½ lb each)
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced (or 1 tsp garlic-infused oil)
  • 2 tsp grated fresh ginger, divided
  • 1 tsp honey (or maple syrup)
  • ½ tsp chili-garlic paste or sriracha (or ¼ tsp red pepper flakes)
  • 2 tsp neutral oil, for searing
  • ½ head napa cabbage, sliced into ribbons
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • Chopped green onions and toasted sesame seeds, for serving
  • 1 cup rice + 1.5 cups water, or leftover rice

Steps

  1. Cook rice if not using leftovers. Preheat oven to 425°F/220°C.
  2. Whisk together soy sauce, rice vinegar, garlic (or garlic-infused oil), 1.5 tsp of the ginger, honey, and chili-garlic paste — this is the glaze.
  3. Pat salmon dry, season lightly with salt and pepper.
  4. Heat neutral oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering (glossy, rippling surface). Sear salmon skin-side up, undisturbed, 3 min until golden.
  5. Flip, brush the top generously with the glaze, then transfer the skillet to the oven. Bake 6 min — done when it flakes easily and looks opaque throughout (145°F/63°C).
  6. While the salmon bakes, melt butter in a separate small pan, add napa cabbage, remaining soy sauce, and remaining ½ tsp ginger. Sauté 2–3 min until wilted but still with a little bite.
  7. Rest the salmon 2 min. Serve over rice with the cabbage alongside, topped with green onions and sesame seeds.

Safety: fish to at least 145°F/63°C, or opaque and flaking easily.

Storage

Best fresh. Leftovers: fridge up to 2 days, eat at home only. Microwave gently ~1 min, or eat cold.

Crispy Baked Chicken Drumsticks

newbatch

Leverages · Superstore chicken drumsticks ($2.49/lb ★)

Serves 4 (2 × 2 meals; ~8 drumsticks) · ~10 min active · ~45 min total · oven-based, hands-off

Source: RecipeTin Eats · 4.73★ (122 ratings). Scaled from 12 to 8 drumsticks.

Modified from source: the original gives no internal-temperature target, only “crispy and golden.” A minimum safe internal temp (165°F/74°C) is added below — a food-safety addition, not a flavor change.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): the rub uses garlic + onion powder (concentrated dried allium). Kept as-written. Lighter option: drop the onion powder and cut the garlic powder to a pinch — the crisp comes from the baking powder + flour, not the allium.

Ingredients

  • ~8 chicken drumsticks
  • 1.5 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • Oil spray
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 3.5 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1.5 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp kosher/cooking salt
  • 3 tbsp plain/all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder (not baking soda)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 390°F/200°C (or 350°F/180°C fan). Line a tray with foil and set a wire rack on top — the rack lets air circulate so the underside crisps instead of steaming.
  2. In a shallow bowl, mix garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, pepper, salt, flour, and baking powder.
  3. Pat the drumsticks very dry (dry skin = crisp skin). Toss in the olive oil, then stretch any loose skin to cover the flesh.
  4. Roll each drumstick in the seasoning mix to coat all over; shake off excess. Arrange on the rack with space between each.
  5. Spray tops generously with oil. Bake 30 min, rotating the tray once halfway.
  6. Increase oven to 430°F/220°C (or 390°F/200°C fan) and bake another 15 min, until deep golden and crisp.
  7. Doneness: thickest part must reach 165°F/74°C and juices run clear. No rest needed.

Safety: chicken to 165°F/74°C at the thickest part.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days. Re-crisp in a 375°F/190°C oven or air fryer ~8–10 min (microwaving works but softens the coating).

Coconut Curry with Tofu & Vegetables

newbatch

Leverages · Superstore tofu, green zucchini, tomatoes; T&T broccoli

Serves ~4 · ~15 min active · ~35 min total · one-pot, reheats and freezes well · meat-free

Source: Minimalist Baker · 4.72★ (189 ratings).

Modified from source: the original is vegetables-only. Pan-fried tofu is added as the protein (to use the $2 tofu deal and eat as a main), and green zucchini is worked into the veg. Browning the tofu first keeps it from turning spongy in the sauce.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): uses 1 small onion + 4 cloves garlic. Lighter option: swap the onion for green-onion tops and use garlic-infused oil instead of minced garlic.

Ingredients

  • 1 block (~350–450g) firm/extra-firm tofu, drained, pressed 10 min, cut into ¾-inch cubes
  • 1 tbsp coconut or olive oil, plus extra for the tofu
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh grated ginger
  • 1 green zucchini, cut into half-moons
  • ½ cup broccoli florets
  • ½ cup diced carrots
  • ¼ cup diced tomato (or a splash of canned)
  • ⅓ cup snow peas (optional)
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 pinch cayenne (optional)
  • 2 × 14-oz cans light coconut milk
  • 1 cup vegetable broth
  • Salt and pepper, to taste · rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Cook rice, or use leftovers.
  2. Brown the tofu first: heat a little oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high until shimmering. Fry the pressed tofu 6–8 min, turning, until golden on most sides. Set aside.
  3. In a large pot, heat 1 tbsp oil over medium. Cook the onion 3–4 min until soft and translucent. Add garlic and ginger; cook 1 min until fragrant.
  4. Add carrots, broccoli, and zucchini; stir 2–3 min. Stir in the curry powder (and cayenne); cook 30 sec until the spices bloom.
  5. Pour in both cans of coconut milk and the broth. Bring to a gentle simmer, cook uncovered 10–15 min until the vegetables are tender and the sauce thickens slightly.
  6. In the last 5 min, stir in the tomato, snow peas, and the browned tofu so it warms through without breaking down. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve over rice.

Safety: no raw-meat concern (tofu is pre-cooked). Bring to a full simmer before serving.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days or freeze up to 3 months. Reheat stovetop over medium-low ~5 min, or microwave covered 2–3 min, stirring halfway.

Citrus Honey-Garlic Shrimp

newfresh-only

Leverages · Superstore shrimp skewers, green onions, oranges

Serves ~3–4 · ~10 min active · ~20 min total · cook fresh — shrimp turns rubbery reheated

Source: Skinnytaste · 4.52★ (118 ratings).

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): uses 1 tbsp honey (a FODMAP to minimize) and 6 cloves garlic. Lighter option: swap the honey for maple syrup and use garlic-infused oil instead of the cloves — the scallion greens are already low-FODMAP.

Ingredients

  • 2 large oranges
  • 1 tbsp honey (or maple syrup — see flag)
  • 2 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp unseasoned rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 32 jumbo shrimp (~18 oz), peeled and deveined (thaw the shrimp skewers first)
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt + black pepper
  • 1 tbsp canola oil, divided
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced (or garlic-infused oil — see flag)
  • 1 tbsp finely minced fresh ginger
  • 2 scallions: whites thinly sliced, greens cut into 2-inch lengths
  • ¼–½ tsp red pepper flakes · cooked rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Zest one orange, then juice both into a bowl. Whisk in honey, soy sauce, vinegar, and a splash of water. Whisk in the cornstarch until smooth — this is your sauce.
  2. Pat shrimp dry; season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat ½ tbsp oil in a skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Cook half the shrimp 1–1½ min per side until pink and opaque; transfer to a plate. Repeat with the rest.
  4. Add remaining oil, then garlic, ginger, scallion whites, and pepper flakes; cook ~30 sec until fragrant (don’t let the garlic brown).
  5. Pour in the sauce and orange zest; cook 1–2 min, stirring, until thick and glossy.
  6. Return the shrimp with the scallion greens; toss 30–60 sec to coat and heat through.
  7. Serve over rice.

Safety: shrimp are done when pink/opaque and curled into a loose “C” (a tight “O” is overcooked); 145°F/63°C if checking.

Storage

Best fresh. Fridge leftovers up to 2 days, eat cold or barely warmed — reheating hard makes shrimp rubbery.

Pork Chop & Black Bean Chili

newbatch

Leverages · Walmart pork chops, vine/canned tomatoes

Serves 4 (2 × 2 meals) · ~15 min active · ~45 min total · one-pot, reheats excellently, freezes well

Source: Budget Bytes · 4.72★ (124 ratings).

Modified from source: protein swapped from ground beef to diced pork chops (this household’s usual cut); bean/tomato/spice quantities scaled up to balance the extra meat, and simmer time extended since diced pork needs longer than ground beef to turn tender.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): onion and garlic. Lighter option: swap the onion for green-onion tops and use garlic-infused oil instead of minced garlic.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced (or green-onion tops)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced (or 1 tbsp garlic-infused oil)
  • 1.5 lb pork sirloin/centre-cut chops (boneless), diced into ½-inch cubes
  • 3 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 lb vine tomatoes, diced (or 1 can diced tomatoes with green chiles)
  • 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1.5 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 cup low-sodium stock or water
  • Optional: corn tortillas, warmed, for serving

Steps

  1. Dice the onion and mince the garlic (or prep swaps). Dice the pork into ½-inch cubes, trimming large fat pieces if you like; season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and garlic (or swaps); sauté 2–3 min until softened and fragrant.
  3. Increase heat to medium-high, add the pork, and brown on all sides, ~5–6 min. It doesn’t need to be cooked through yet — just browned outside.
  4. Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and oregano; cook 30 sec until fragrant (the spices “bloom”).
  5. Add tomatoes, black beans, tomato paste, salt, and stock. Stir to combine.
  6. Bring to a gentle boil, reduce to low, cover partially, and simmer 25–30 min, stirring occasionally, until the pork is tender (shreds easily) and the chili thickens.
  7. Taste, adjust salt/pepper. Serve hot, with warmed corn tortillas if desired.

Safety: pork to at least 145°F/63°C — the long simmer takes it well past; judge by texture (shreds easily).

Storage

Fridge 4–5 days, or freeze up to 3 months. Reheat stovetop ~5 min, or microwave covered 2–3 min, stirring halfway.

Laksa Noodle Soup

MBKK 5★batch

Leverages · Superstore chicken drumsticks ($2.49/lb ★, stock), ginger; optional: Superstore tofu ($2.00) subbed for tofu puffs

Serves 2 large bowls · ~15 min active · ~1 hr total · broth reheats well, keep noodles separate

Source: RecipeTin Eats · 4.97★ (340 ratings). Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin — already in the repo (recipes/laksa-noodle-soup.md). Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (high): 2 cloves fresh garlic plus store-bought laksa paste, which itself typically contains onion/garlic — a real, likely high FODMAP load. Lighter option: swap the garlic for garlic-infused oil; check the laksa paste's ingredient list for where onion/garlic falls.

Chicken Stock

  • 2 cups (500 ml) chicken stock/broth
  • 1 cup (250 ml) water
  • 3 chicken drumsticks

Laksa Broth

  • 1½ tbsp oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced (or garlic-infused oil — see flag)
  • 2 cm ginger, finely grated
  • 1 lemongrass stalk, white part grated
  • 2 bird's eye chillies, finely chopped
  • ½ cup (135 g) laksa paste
  • 400 ml can coconut milk
  • 2 tsp fish sauce

Noodles + Toppings

  • 50 g dried vermicelli noodles
  • 100 g hokkien noodles (optional)
  • 80 g bean sprouts
  • 80 g tofu puffs, cut in half (or pan-fried cubes of Superstore firm tofu, see Leverages)

Laksa Chilli Sauce

  • ½ tsp sugar
  • 1½ tsp soy sauce
  • ½ garlic clove, minced
  • 1½ tsp laksa paste
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli paste from jar
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Garnishes

  • Fresh coriander/cilantro
  • Lime wedges
  • Crispy fried shallots (optional)
  • Finely sliced red chilli (optional)

Steps

  1. Chicken stock: place the stock ingredients in a medium saucepan over high heat; bring to a simmer, then reduce to medium-high. Cook 25 min, until the chicken flesh falls easily from the bone and the liquid has reduced by about a third. Discard the skin; pull the flesh into a bowl; discard the bones. Set the broth aside.
  2. Laksa chilli sauce: mix all ingredients in a small bowl. Set aside at least 20 min.
  3. Laksa broth: heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium-low heat. Add garlic and ginger; sauté 20 seconds until fragrant (don't let it brown). Add the lemongrass and chillies; cook 1 min.
  4. Add the laksa paste; increase heat to medium and cook 2 min, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
  5. Add the chicken stock, coconut milk, fish sauce, and 2 tsp of the laksa chilli sauce. Cover and simmer 10 min.
  6. Taste and adjust — lime juice for sourness, fish sauce for saltiness. Add the tofu puffs (or fried tofu). Turn off heat, leave covered 5 min.
  7. Cook the noodles per packet directions. Divide between 2 bowls; top with shredded chicken. Ladle the hot broth over; top with bean sprouts and garnishes. Serve with the laksa chilli sauce on the side.

Safety: chicken to at least 165°F/74°C — the 25-min simmer well exceeds this; judge by the flesh falling easily from the bone.

Storage

Fridge broth and chicken up to 3–4 days; store noodles/bean sprouts separately to avoid sogginess. Reheat broth on the stovetop or microwave until steaming, then combine with noodles at serving time.

Crispy Pan-Fried Lemongrass Chicken Thighs

MBKK 5★fresh-only

Serves 2 (4 bone-in, skin-on thighs) · ~10 min active + 1 hr marinate · ~25 min cook · best fresh, skin won’t stay crispy reheated

Source: I Am A Food Blog · no star rating shown on site. Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin. Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (high): the marinade uses 1 small shallot + 2 cloves garlic — concentrated onion-family aromatics. Lighter option: swap for 1–2 tbsp garlic-infused oil plus a pinch of green-onion tops.

Ingredients

  • 1 stalk lemongrass, white part only, finely minced
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • ¼–½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • Oil for the pan
  • Steamed rice, to serve; lettuce and tomato, optional

Steps

  1. Combine the minced lemongrass, shallot, garlic, sugar, fish sauce, and black pepper in a shallow bowl or ziplock bag.
  2. Add the chicken thighs and coat thoroughly. Marinate at least 1 hour, or overnight for more flavor.
  3. Cook rice, timed to finish with the chicken.
  4. Heat a thin layer of oil in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat until it shimmers (moves easily, faint shimmer).
  5. Lay the thighs skin-side down. Cook 15–20 min without moving, until the skin is deep golden-brown and crispy — don’t flip early, the skin needs the full time to render.
  6. Flip and cook ~5 min more, checking near the bone for doneness (~25 min total).
  7. Serve over rice with lettuce and tomato if desired.

Safety: chicken to 165°F/74°C at the thickest part near the bone — bone-in thighs can look done outside before the middle is.

Storage

Best fresh. If storing, fridge up to 3 days; re-crisp in a hot dry pan or toaster oven, not the microwave.

One-Pan “Marry Me” Chicken Orzo

MBKK 5★batch

Serves 6 · ~15 min active · ~35 min total · grain-and-sauce combo, reheats well

Source: Kale Junkie · 4.77★ (135 ratings). Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin. Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (high): 4–5 cloves mashed garlic is a substantial dose. Lighter option: swap for 2–3 tbsp garlic-infused oil in the sauté step — this mutes the “marry me” garlic punch, a real tradeoff.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cubed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4–5 cloves garlic, mashed
  • ¾ cup sun-dried tomatoes (packed in oil, drained)
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp kosher salt · 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup orzo
  • 2¾ cups chicken broth or water
  • ¾ cup full-fat coconut milk or heavy cream
  • 2 large handfuls fresh spinach
  • ¾ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Pat the chicken dry and cut into cubes.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high; add the olive oil.
  3. Once the oil shimmers, add the chicken; cook 2–3 min until the edges turn opaque and lightly browned.
  4. Add the sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, Italian seasoning, paprika, salt, and pepper; cook 3–4 min, stirring, until fragrant and the chicken is mostly cooked through.
  5. Add the orzo and broth; stir, scraping up any browned bits.
  6. Reduce to medium, cover, cook 12 min, stirring occasionally so the orzo doesn’t stick.
  7. Once the orzo is tender, stir in the coconut milk (or cream) and spinach until the spinach wilts, 1–2 min.
  8. Stir in the Parmesan until melted. Serve immediately.

Safety: chicken to 165°F/74°C — check a larger cube for no pink and clear juices.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days. Orzo drinks up liquid — reheat with a splash of broth or water stirred in to loosen the sauce.

Thai Chilli Basil Chicken

MBKK 5★batch

Serves 2 · ~10 min active · ~15 min total · reheats well as a stir-fry over rice

Source: RecipeTin Eats · 4.93★ (163 ratings). Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin. Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): 2 large garlic cloves plus the white of the green onion carry fructans (green tops are fine). Lighter option: use 1 tbsp garlic-infused oil and only the green onion tops.

Ingredients

  • 225 g (7 oz) skinless, boneless chicken thigh, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 green onion, cut into 4 cm (2") lengths
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves, loosely packed (Holy Basil if available)
  • 2 large garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 bird’s eye or Thai chilli, deseeded and finely chopped
  • 1½ tbsp oil (peanut, vegetable, or canola)
  • Sauce: 2 tsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp light soy sauce, 1 tsp dark soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar, 2 tbsp water
  • Steamed jasmine rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Combine the sauce ingredients in a small bowl; set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok or pan over high heat until it shimmers.
  3. Add the garlic and chilli; stir constantly 10 sec until fragrant — don’t let the garlic brown.
  4. Add the white part of the green onion and the chicken; stir-fry ~2 min until cooked through (no pink, juices run clear).
  5. Add the sauce; cook ~1 min until it reduces to a thick, glossy coating.
  6. Toss in the green onion tops and basil; stir just until the basil wilts, 15–20 sec.
  7. Serve immediately over jasmine rice.

Safety: chicken to 165°F/74°C — at this small dice, 2 min of high heat is enough; check for no pink.

Storage

Fridge up to 3–4 days. Reheat in a hot pan or microwave 1–2 min; the basil darkens slightly but flavor holds.
