Chicken & Yu Choy Chop Suey

unratedbatch

Leverages · T&T yu-choy sum ($1.98/lb ★) + chicken breast (Superstore $6.88/lb ★ or Walmart $6.79/lb ★)

Serves 4 (2 × 2 meals) · ~15 min active · ~40 min total (incl. 20 min velveting rest) · wok stir-fry, reheats very well

Source: RecipeTin Eats · 4.94★ (137 ratings). From the repo (recipes/chicken-yu-choy-rice-bowls.md), unrated/never cooked.

No substitution needed this week — yu choy itself is finally the flyer deal, a direct match to the recipe as written (unlike prior weeks, which subbed in green beans, broccoli, or bok choy).

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): onion, garlic, and regular (button/cremini) mushrooms are all notable triggers. Lighter option: swap the onion for green-onion tops, use garlic-infused oil instead of minced garlic, and use oyster or canned mushrooms (or omit) instead of button/cremini.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (340g) boneless, skinless chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp baking soda (for velveting)
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry, or mirin)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Dash of white pepper
  • 1½ cups water
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil, divided
  • 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped (or 1 tbsp garlic-infused oil — see flag)
  • 1 small onion, sliced (or the green tops of 3–4 green onions — see flag)
  • 12–14 stems yu choy, stems and leafy tops separated, cut into 2–3 inch pieces
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms (oyster mushrooms preferred — see flag)
  • 2 cups bean sprouts
  • 1½ cups jasmine rice (dry) + 2¼ cups water

Equipment

Wok or large skillet, pot for rice (or rice cooker), small mixing bowl, cutting board, sharp knife, meat thermometer (recommended)

Steps

  1. Velvet the chicken. Toss the sliced chicken with the baking soda in a bowl and let it sit 20 min — this tenderizes the meat, a classic Chinese-restaurant trick. Rinse thoroughly under cold water and pat dry.
  2. Start the rice. Rinse the rice under cold water until it runs mostly clear. Add to a pot with the water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil uncovered, then cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer 15 min. Turn off heat and let sit covered 5 more min, then fluff.
  3. Make the sauce. Whisk the cornstarch into the soy sauce until no lumps remain, then stir in the oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, sesame oil, white pepper, and water.
  4. Cut the yu choy into 2–3 inch pieces, keeping the thicker stems separate from the leafy tops (stems need slightly more cooking time).
  5. Heat 1½ tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat until it shimmers (the surface looks glossy and moves in ripples).
  6. Add the garlic (or garlic-infused oil); stir 10–15 sec until fragrant, then add the onion (or green onion tops) and cook 1 min until just softened.
  7. Add the chicken in a single layer; cook 1–2 min until the surface turns opaque white.
  8. Add the yu choy stems, carrots, and mushrooms; stir-fry 1–2 min.
  9. Add the yu choy leaves, bean sprouts, and the sauce; toss 1–2 min until the sauce thickens to a glossy coating. Doneness check: chicken must reach 165°F/74°C at the thickest piece — no rest needed at this temp.
  10. Serve over rice.

Safety: chicken must reach a minimum internal temperature of 165°F/74°C, checked with a meat thermometer at the thickest piece.

Storage

Refrigerate up to 4 days. Reheat covered in the microwave 2 min, stir, then 30–60 sec more until steaming hot throughout.

Beef & Bok Choy Stir-Fry

unratedbatch

Leverages · Superstore beef sirloin tip steak or roast ($11/lb ★, grass fed, pasture raised) + Superstore kale ($1.99/lb ★, swapped for bok choy)

Serves 4 (2 × 2 meals) · ~15 min active · ~40 min total (incl. 20 min velveting rest) · wok stir-fry, reheats very well

Source: RecipeTin Eats · 4.93★ (149 ratings). From the repo (recipes/beef-bok-choy-stir-fry.md), unrated/never cooked.

Modified from source: no bok choy deal turned up this week, so kale stands in — separate stems from leaves as usual, add the stems with the carrots (give them slightly longer than bok choy stems since kale is heartier), leaves at the end just to wilt.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): onion and garlic. Lighter option: swap the onion for the green tops of green onion and use garlic-infused oil in place of the minced garlic.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz (280g) beef sirloin tip, sliced thin against the grain
  • ¾ tsp baking soda (for velveting)
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1½ tbsp oyster sauce
  • 3 tbsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry, or mirin)
  • 1½ tsp sugar
  • ¾ tsp toasted sesame oil
  • Pinch of white pepper
  • 1½ tbsp cornstarch + ¾ cup water (slurry)
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil (peanut or vegetable), divided
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced (or 1 tsp garlic-infused oil — see flag)
  • ½ small onion, sliced (or the green tops of 2 green onions — see flag)
  • ½ red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1–2 carrots, sliced diagonally
  • 4–5 cups kale, stems separated from leaves, stems sliced thin (swap for bok choy)
  • 3 green onions, cut into 1.5 inch pieces
  • 1½ cups rice (dry) + 2¼ cups water

Equipment

Large skillet or wok, pot for rice (or rice cooker), small mixing bowl, cutting board, sharp knife, meat thermometer (optional — thin strips cook mainly by sight/texture)

Steps

  1. Velvet the beef. Slice thinly against the grain (across the visible muscle lines). Toss with the baking soda and let sit 20 min — this tenderizes the meat. Rinse under cold water and pat dry.
  2. Start the rice. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear. Add to a pot with the water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil uncovered, cover, reduce to low, simmer 15 min. Turn off heat, sit covered 5 min, then fluff.
  3. In a bowl, combine the soy sauce, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, sugar, sesame oil, and white pepper. Set aside 2 tbsp of this mixture and toss it with the beef to marinate briefly.
  4. Whisk the cornstarch into the remaining sauce, then whisk in the water to make a thickening slurry.
  5. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat until it shimmers (surface looks glossy and ripples). Add the garlic (or garlic-infused oil) and onion (or green onion tops); stir-fry 30 sec until fragrant.
  6. Add the beef in a single layer. Let it sit undisturbed 1 min, then stir-fry 30–60 sec until browned outside but not fully cooked through. Remove to a plate.
  7. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Add the carrots, bell pepper, and kale stems; stir-fry 2–3 min until just tender (a minute or so longer than bok choy stems would need — pierce with a fork to check).
  8. Pour in the slurry and stir until it bubbles and thickens, about 1 min.
  9. Return the beef along with the kale leaves and green onion pieces. Toss 45–60 sec until the leaves wilt and the beef is cooked through. Doneness check: thin strips cook in well under a minute at high heat — judge by color (browned throughout, no red remaining).
  10. Serve over rice.

Safety: whole-muscle beef (not ground) is safe at 145°F/63°C with a 3-min rest. For strips this thin, judge by color/texture since they cook in seconds.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days. Reheat covered in the microwave 2 min, stir, then 30–60 sec more.

Pan-Seared Salmon with Napa Cabbage

unratedfresh-only

Leverages · T&T Atlantic salmon fillet ($11.97/lb). Napa cabbage isn't a flyer feature but is a cheap year-round staple at T&T

Serves 2 · ~25 min total · fresh dinner only — don't batch for office lunches (strong fish smell doesn't suit shared microwaves)

Source: WellPlated · 4.54★ (267 ratings). From the repo (recipes/salmon-napa-cabbage.md), unrated/never cooked.

Modified from source: the napa cabbage side (this household's usual pairing) is added, sautéed separately in butter, soy, and ginger. The source's 1 lb fillet is this household's usual 2 steaks at about ½ lb each.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): garlic and honey are both notable triggers. Lighter option: swap the honey for maple syrup and use garlic-infused oil in place of the minced garlic.

Ingredients

  • 2 Atlantic salmon fillets (about ½ lb each), at room temperature
  • 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced (or 1 tsp garlic-infused oil — see flag)
  • 2 tsp grated fresh ginger, divided
  • 1 tsp honey (or maple syrup — see flag)
  • ½ tsp chili-garlic paste or sriracha (or ¼ tsp red pepper flakes)
  • 2 tsp neutral oil, for searing
  • ½ head napa cabbage (short variety), sliced into thin ribbons
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • Chopped green onions and toasted sesame seeds, for serving
  • 1 cup rice (dry) + 1.5 cups water, or leftover rice from earlier in the week

Equipment

Oven-safe nonstick or cast iron skillet, a second small skillet (for the cabbage), cutting board, knife, small mixing bowl, meat thermometer (optional)

Steps

  1. If not using leftover rice, cook 1 cup rice: rinse, add to a pot with 1.5 cups water and a pinch of salt, boil uncovered, cover, simmer 15 min on low, rest covered 5 min, fluff.
  2. Preheat the oven to 425°F/220°C.
  3. Make the glaze. Whisk together the soy sauce, rice vinegar, garlic (or garlic-infused oil), 1.5 tsp of the ginger, honey, and chili-garlic paste.
  4. Pat the salmon dry and season lightly with salt and pepper.
  5. Heat the neutral oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high until it shimmers (glossy, moves in ripples). Place the salmon skin-side up and sear undisturbed 3 min, until golden.
  6. Flip the salmon, brush the top generously with the glaze, then transfer the whole skillet to the oven. Bake 6 min. Doneness check: salmon flakes easily with a fork and looks opaque all the way through, or reaches 145°F/63°C at the thickest part.
  7. While the salmon bakes, melt the butter in a separate small pan over medium heat. Add the napa cabbage, the remaining 1 tsp soy sauce, and the remaining ½ tsp ginger. Sauté 2–3 min, tossing often, until the cabbage wilts but keeps a little bite.
  8. Rest the salmon 2 min. Serve over rice with the napa cabbage alongside, topped with green onions and sesame seeds.

Safety: fish should reach a minimum internal temperature of 145°F/63°C, or be opaque and flake easily with a fork.

Storage

Best eaten fresh the night it's cooked. Any leftovers: fridge up to 2 days and eat at home only — reheated fish smells strong for shared office microwaves. Reheat gently, covered, ~1 min, or eat cold over a salad.

Greek Sheet Pan Chicken

newbatch

Leverages · chicken breast (Superstore $6.88/lb ★ or Walmart $6.79/lb ★); tomatoes (Superstore Roma $1.99/lb ★ or T&T hot house $1.77/lb)

Serves 4 (2 × 2 meals) · ~15 min active · ~45 min total · sheet-pan, hands-off

Source: Downshiftology · 4.96★ (806 ratings).

Modified from source: the original calls for 6 bone-in chicken thighs; 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (~1.5 lb) stand in to match this week's deal, scaled down slightly to a 4-serving batch. Breast cooks faster than thigh — start checking temperature at the 20-minute mark of the chicken's bake time rather than waiting the full 30.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): 4 garlic cloves + ½ red onion in the marinade. Lighter option: swap the garlic for 2 tbsp garlic-infused oil and use only the green tops of green onion in place of the red onion.

Marinade

  • ½ cup olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced (~3 tbsp)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced (or 2 tbsp garlic-infused oil — see flag)
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Sheet Pan

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (~1.5 lb)
  • 1 medium zucchini, halved lengthwise and sliced
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, chopped into 1-inch pieces
  • ½ large red onion, thinly sliced into wedges (or green-onion tops — see flag)
  • 1 pint cherry/grape tomatoes, or an equivalent weight of the on-sale Roma/hot house tomatoes, quartered
  • ½ cup kalamata olives, pitted
  • ¼ cup feta cheese
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley

Equipment

Rimmed baking sheet, small mixing bowl, large bowl, meat thermometer

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F/220°C. Whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic (or garlic-infused oil), oregano, thyme, Dijon mustard, salt, and pepper.
  2. Place the chicken breasts in a bowl and pour two-thirds of the marinade over them. Toss to coat and let marinate 10–15 min at room temperature.
  3. Spread the zucchini, bell pepper, onion, and tomatoes on a baking sheet. Drizzle the remaining marinade over top and toss to coat.
  4. Nestle the chicken breasts among the vegetables on the sheet pan. Bake 20 min, then start checking — breast cooks faster than the source recipe's thighs.
  5. Remove from the oven, sprinkle the olives and feta over top, then return to the oven 8–12 min more until the chicken reaches 165°F/74°C internally. Doneness check: juices run clear and the centre is no longer pink.
  6. Top with fresh parsley before serving.

Safety: chicken breast to a minimum internal temperature of 165°F/74°C at the thickest part.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days. Reheat covered in the microwave 2 min, or in a 350°F/180°C oven ~10 min.

Oven-Baked BBQ Pork Ribs

newbatch

Leverages · Walmart fresh pork rib rack ($3.98/lb ★)

Serves 4–6 (scale to ~1 rack, ~2.5–3 lb) · ~15 min active · ~2.5 hr total, mostly hands-off oven time

Source: RecipeTin Eats · 4.98★ (274 ratings). Followed as written; rub and sauce quantities below make plenty even for a single rack.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): garlic powder + onion powder in both the rub and the sauce. Lighter option: halve the powders and skip the onion powder — the paprika/cayenne/herb base carries most of the flavor.

Barbecue Rub

  • 2½ tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1½ tsp onion powder (see flag)
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1½ tsp dried thyme
  • 1½ tsp dried oregano
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Ribs

  • 1 rack pork side ribs (~2.5–3 lb)
  • ¾ cup apple juice (or sweet apple cider)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Homemade Barbecue Sauce

  • ½ cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1½ cups tomato ketchup
  • ½ cup water
  • 1½ tbsp molasses
  • ⅓ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tsp mustard powder
  • 1½ tsp garlic powder (see flag)
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt · 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp Tabasco or cayenne (optional)

Equipment

Rimmed baking tray, foil, small saucepan, small bowl for rub

Steps

  1. Combine the rub ingredients. Rub thoroughly over both sides of the ribs; marinate at least 20 min at room temperature, or overnight in the fridge.
  2. Preheat oven to 320°F/160°C.
  3. Place the ribs in a single layer on a tray. Pour the apple juice underneath, cover tightly with foil, and bake 1 hr 30 min until the meat is tender.
  4. Meanwhile, make the sauce: combine all sauce ingredients in a small saucepan and simmer over medium heat ~45 min, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
  5. Remove ribs from the oven, increase oven temp to 350°F/180°C. Uncover, drizzle with olive oil, and bake 15 min more, uncovered, until the rub turns crusty.
  6. Line a fresh tray with foil and parchment. Transfer the ribs to it, pouring the pan juices over top. Flip bone-side up, slather with barbecue sauce, and bake 10 min.
  7. Flip meat-side up, slather with more sauce, bake 5 min. Repeat slathering and baking 2–3 more times for a thick, sticky glaze.
  8. Cut into portions and serve with the remaining sauce on the side.

Safety: pork to at least 145°F/63°C — the long braise takes it well past this; judge by tenderness (meat pulls easily from the bone).

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days, sliced into portions. Reheat covered in a 350°F/180°C oven ~10 min, or microwave covered 2 min.

Mexican Ground Beef Skillet

newbatch

Leverages · Superstore ground beef ($9/lb)

Serves 4 (scaled down from the source's 6) · ~10 min active · ~30 min total · one-pan skillet, reheats well

Source: Fit Foodie Finds · 4.90★ (288 ratings). Followed as written, scaled to 4 servings.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): 1 tbsp minced garlic + ½ yellow onion. Lighter option: swap the garlic for garlic-infused oil and use only the green tops of green onion in place of the yellow onion.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic (or garlic-infused oil — see flag)
  • ½ medium yellow onion, minced (or green-onion tops — see flag)
  • 2 large bell peppers, diced
  • 1 lb ground beef (85% lean)
  • 3 tbsp taco seasoning (homemade or store-bought)
  • ½ cup tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp diced green chiles
  • 15 oz canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup quick-cooking brown or white rice
  • 1.5 cups beef broth
  • ½ cup shredded Mexican cheese blend

Equipment

Large nonstick skillet (or deep Dutch oven)

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil over medium-high heat in a large skillet until fragrant.
  2. Add the garlic (or garlic-infused oil), onion (or green-onion tops), and bell pepper; sauté 3–5 min until partially cooked.
  3. Push the vegetables to the side and add the ground beef. Cook 7–9 min until fully browned, then mix everything together.
  4. Stir in the taco seasoning, tomato sauce, green chiles, and black beans; cook a few minutes to combine.
  5. Add the rice and beef broth; bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
  6. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer ~20 min until the rice is tender and has absorbed the liquid.
  7. Top with cheese; cover off-heat 2–3 min until melted, or broil briefly if a browned top is wanted.

Safety: ground beef must reach 160°F/71°C — judge by no pink remaining and clear juices when checking a crumble.

Storage

Fridge up to 3–5 days in an airtight container, or freeze up to 3 months. Reheat covered in the microwave, or warm in a 350°F/180°C oven, adding a splash of broth if it's dried out.

Classic Smash Burgers

MBKK 5★fresh-only

Serves 4 double-patty burgers (2 people × 2 burgers) · ~18 min active · ~30 min total · best eaten fresh — not a reheat/transport dish

Source: Natasha's Kitchen · 4.98★ (325 ratings). Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin. Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (high): the red onion topping and the wheat bun are the notable triggers here. Lighter option: swap the red onion for pickled onion in a small amount or the green tops of green onions, and swap the brioche bun for a gluten-free bun or a lettuce wrap. Garlic powder in the patty seasoning is optional and can be omitted.

Patties

  • 1½ lb ground beef (80/20), divided into 8 portions (~3 oz each)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Garlic powder, optional (see FODMAP note)
  • 4 slices medium cheddar cheese

Sauce

  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard

Toppings

  • 4 burger buns (brioche recommended)
  • 2 cups shredded iceberg lettuce
  • 1 large tomato, sliced
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced (see FODMAP note)
  • 2 dill pickles, cut into 12 slices total

Equipment

Griddle or cast-iron skillet, burger press (or a sturdy flat-bottomed object), parchment paper squares, metal spatula, mixing bowl

Steps

  1. Divide the ground beef into 8 equal portions (~3 oz each) and roll into loose balls. Cover and refrigerate until ready to cook — keeping the beef cold helps it hold together while smashing.
  2. Stir the mayonnaise and mustard together in a small bowl for the sauce; set aside.
  3. Prep all toppings ahead of time: shred the lettuce, slice the tomato, thinly slice the onion, and slice the pickles.
  4. Butter the cut sides of the buns and toast over medium heat in a dry skillet until golden brown, 1–2 minutes. Set aside.
  5. Heat the griddle or skillet over medium-high heat until visibly hot (a drop of water should sizzle and evaporate almost instantly). Place 2–4 beef balls on the hot surface, spaced apart. Immediately lay a square of parchment paper over each ball and firmly press down with a burger press (or a flat, heavy object) to smash into a thin patty, about ¼-inch thick.
  6. Peel off the parchment. Season the exposed side with salt, pepper, and garlic powder if using. Add ½ tsp of the mayo-mustard sauce on top. Cook undisturbed for about 2 minutes, until the bottom is deeply browned and crisp at the edges — you'll see the edges turn dark and lacy.
  7. Flip each patty and cook 1 minute more. Top half of the patties with a slice of cheddar, then stack the other patty on top (cheese side in) so each burger is a double patty with melted cheese in the middle.
  8. Assemble: spread sauce on the bottom bun, then layer pickles, lettuce, tomato, and onion. Add the double patty stack and the top bun.

Safety: ground beef must reach a minimum internal temperature of 160°F/71°C. Smash-cooked patties are thin and cooked hot and fast, so by the time both sides are well-browned they're reliably past this point — texture (firm, no pink) is the practical doneness cue since patties are too thin to probe reliably.

Storage

Best assembled and eaten immediately — this isn't a leftovers or lunch-transport dish (crispy patty, soft bun, fresh toppings). If you do have leftover cooked patties, store them separately from the buns/toppings in the fridge up to 2 days and reheat in a hot dry skillet, ~1 minute per side, then rebuild the burger fresh.

Crispy Pan-Fried Lemongrass Chicken Thighs

MBKK 5★fresh-only

Serves 2 (4 bone-in, skin-on thighs) · ~10 min active + 1 hr marinate · ~25 min cook · best fresh, skin won’t stay crispy reheated

Source: I Am A Food Blog · no star rating shown on site. Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin. Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (high): the marinade uses 1 small shallot + 2 cloves garlic — concentrated onion-family aromatics. Lighter option: swap for 1–2 tbsp garlic-infused oil plus a pinch of green-onion tops.

Ingredients

  • 1 stalk lemongrass, white part only, finely minced
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • ¼–½ tsp ground black pepper
  • 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • Oil for the pan
  • Steamed rice, to serve; lettuce and tomato, optional

Steps

  1. Combine the minced lemongrass, shallot, garlic, sugar, fish sauce, and black pepper in a shallow bowl or ziplock bag.
  2. Add the chicken thighs and coat thoroughly. Marinate at least 1 hour, or overnight for more flavor.
  3. Cook rice, timed to finish with the chicken.
  4. Heat a thin layer of oil in a heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat until it shimmers (moves easily, faint shimmer).
  5. Lay the thighs skin-side down. Cook 15–20 min without moving, until the skin is deep golden-brown and crispy — don’t flip early, the skin needs the full time to render.
  6. Flip and cook ~5 min more, checking near the bone for doneness (~25 min total).
  7. Serve over rice with lettuce and tomato if desired.

Safety: chicken to 165°F/74°C at the thickest part near the bone — bone-in thighs can look done outside before the middle is.

Storage

Best fresh. If storing, fridge up to 3 days; re-crisp in a hot dry pan or toaster oven, not the microwave.

Laksa Noodle Soup

MBKK 5★batch

Serves 2 large bowls · ~15 min active · ~1 hr total · broth reheats well, keep noodles separate

Source: RecipeTin Eats · 4.97★ (340 ratings). Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin — in the repo (recipes/laksa-noodle-soup.md). Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (high): 2 cloves fresh garlic plus store-bought laksa paste, which itself typically contains onion/garlic — a real, likely high FODMAP load. Lighter option: swap the garlic for garlic-infused oil; check the laksa paste's ingredient list for where onion/garlic falls.

Chicken Stock

  • 2 cups (500 ml) chicken stock/broth
  • 1 cup (250 ml) water
  • 3 chicken drumsticks

Laksa Broth

  • 1½ tbsp oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced (or garlic-infused oil — see flag)
  • 2 cm ginger, finely grated
  • 1 lemongrass stalk, white part grated
  • 2 bird's eye chillies, finely chopped
  • ½ cup (135 g) laksa paste
  • 400 ml can coconut milk
  • 2 tsp fish sauce

Noodles + Toppings

  • 50 g dried vermicelli noodles
  • 100 g hokkien noodles (optional)
  • 80 g bean sprouts
  • 80 g tofu puffs, cut in half (or pan-fried cubes of firm tofu)

Laksa Chilli Sauce

  • ½ tsp sugar
  • 1½ tsp soy sauce
  • ½ garlic clove, minced
  • 1½ tsp laksa paste
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli paste from jar
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Garnishes

  • Fresh coriander/cilantro
  • Lime wedges
  • Crispy fried shallots (optional)
  • Finely sliced red chilli (optional)

Steps

  1. Chicken stock: place the stock ingredients in a medium saucepan over high heat; bring to a simmer, then reduce to medium-high. Cook 25 min, until the chicken flesh falls easily from the bone and the liquid has reduced by about a third. Discard the skin; pull the flesh into a bowl; discard the bones. Set the broth aside.
  2. Laksa chilli sauce: mix all ingredients in a small bowl. Set aside at least 20 min.
  3. Laksa broth: heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium-low heat. Add garlic and ginger; sauté 20 seconds until fragrant (don't let it brown). Add the lemongrass and chillies; cook 1 min.
  4. Add the laksa paste; increase heat to medium and cook 2 min, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
  5. Add the chicken stock, coconut milk, fish sauce, and 2 tsp of the laksa chilli sauce. Cover and simmer 10 min.
  6. Taste and adjust — lime juice for sourness, fish sauce for saltiness. Add the tofu puffs (or fried tofu). Turn off heat, leave covered 5 min.
  7. Cook the noodles per packet directions. Divide between 2 bowls; top with shredded chicken. Ladle the hot broth over; top with bean sprouts and garnishes. Serve with the laksa chilli sauce on the side.

Safety: chicken to at least 165°F/74°C — the 25-min simmer well exceeds this; judge by the flesh falling easily from the bone.

Storage

Fridge broth and chicken up to 3–4 days; store noodles/bean sprouts separately to avoid sogginess. Reheat broth on the stovetop or microwave until steaming, then combine with noodles at serving time.

One-Pan “Marry Me” Chicken Orzo

MBKK 5★batch

Serves 6 · ~15 min active · ~35 min total · grain-and-sauce combo, reheats well

Source: Kale Junkie · 4.77★ (135 ratings). Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin. Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (high): 4–5 cloves mashed garlic is a substantial dose. Lighter option: swap for 2–3 tbsp garlic-infused oil in the sauté step — this mutes the “marry me” garlic punch, a real tradeoff.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cubed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4–5 cloves garlic, mashed
  • ¾ cup sun-dried tomatoes (packed in oil, drained)
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp kosher salt · 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup orzo
  • 2¾ cups chicken broth or water
  • ¾ cup full-fat coconut milk or heavy cream
  • 2 large handfuls fresh spinach
  • ¾ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Pat the chicken dry and cut into cubes.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high; add the olive oil.
  3. Once the oil shimmers, add the chicken; cook 2–3 min until the edges turn opaque and lightly browned.
  4. Add the sun-dried tomatoes, garlic, Italian seasoning, paprika, salt, and pepper; cook 3–4 min, stirring, until fragrant and the chicken is mostly cooked through.
  5. Add the orzo and broth; stir, scraping up any browned bits.
  6. Reduce to medium, cover, cook 12 min, stirring occasionally so the orzo doesn’t stick.
  7. Once the orzo is tender, stir in the coconut milk (or cream) and spinach until the spinach wilts, 1–2 min.
  8. Stir in the Parmesan until melted. Serve immediately.

Safety: chicken to 165°F/74°C — check a larger cube for no pink and clear juices.

Storage

Fridge up to 4 days. Orzo drinks up liquid — reheat with a splash of broth or water stirred in to loosen the sauce.

Thai Chilli Basil Chicken

MBKK 5★batch

Serves 2 · ~10 min active · ~15 min total · reheats well as a stir-fry over rice

Source: RecipeTin Eats · 4.93★ (163 ratings). Cooked and scored 5★ by Kevin. Followed as written.

⚠️ FODMAP flag (moderate): 2 large garlic cloves plus the white of the green onion carry fructans (green tops are fine). Lighter option: use 1 tbsp garlic-infused oil and only the green onion tops.

Ingredients

  • 225 g (7 oz) skinless, boneless chicken thigh, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 green onion, cut into 4 cm (2") lengths
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves, loosely packed (Holy Basil if available)
  • 2 large garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 bird’s eye or Thai chilli, deseeded and finely chopped
  • 1½ tbsp oil (peanut, vegetable, or canola)
  • Sauce: 2 tsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp light soy sauce, 1 tsp dark soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar, 2 tbsp water
  • Steamed jasmine rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Combine the sauce ingredients in a small bowl; set aside.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok or pan over high heat until it shimmers.
  3. Add the garlic and chilli; stir constantly 10 sec until fragrant — don’t let the garlic brown.
  4. Add the white part of the green onion and the chicken; stir-fry ~2 min until cooked through (no pink, juices run clear).
  5. Add the sauce; cook ~1 min until it reduces to a thick, glossy coating.
  6. Toss in the green onion tops and basil; stir just until the basil wilts, 15–20 sec.
  7. Serve immediately over jasmine rice.

Safety: chicken to 165°F/74°C — at this small dice, 2 min of high heat is enough; check for no pink.

Storage

Fridge up to 3–4 days. Reheat in a hot pan or microwave 1–2 min; the basil darkens slightly but flavor holds.
